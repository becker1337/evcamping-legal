## Contact

Found a bug, have a question, or need help with your account? Email **becker1337@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you.

You can also report a bug directly from the app: shake your device at any time, or go to **Profile → Settings → Report a Bug**.

## Common questions

**How do I delete my account?**
Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and personal data.

**How do I change the app's language?**
Profile → Settings → Language. EVCamping supports English, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Polish, Ukrainian, Romanian, and Dutch.

**A spot's info looks wrong or outdated.**
Open the spot and use the report option, or email us with the spot name and what's incorrect.
