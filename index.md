# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2026_

## What we collect

**Account info:** your email address and password (handled by our backend provider, Supabase — we never see or store your password ourselves).

**Location:** your device's current location, used only to center the map and show distances to nearby camping spots — we don't store a history of where you've been.

**Photos:** any photo you choose to upload for your profile, a spot, or a review.

**Content you submit:** spot suggestions, reviews, ratings, notes, and bug reports, along with your vehicle model if you provide it during setup.

## How we use it

To run the app's core features: showing you the map, letting you save and review spots, and letting other users see spots and reviews you've chosen to submit. Location is used live, on-device, for the current session — it is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking. We don't use your data for targeted advertising.

## What other users can see

Your display name, your submitted spots, reviews, and photos are visible to other users of the app, the same way they'd appear on any community map. Your email address is never shown to other users.

## Where your data lives

We use Supabase, a third-party backend provider, to store account, spot, and review data, and to host uploaded photos. Supabase acts as our data processor and does not use your data for its own purposes.

## Content moderation

Reviews and photos can be reported by users for objectionable content. Reported content past a threshold is automatically hidden pending review by an app administrator, who can restore or permanently remove it.

## Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and personal data; community content you contributed (reviews, spot submissions, photos) is kept but anonymized rather than deleted outright, the same way a removed contributor's edits stay on a shared map. You can also edit or remove your own submitted content at any time before deleting your account.

## Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
